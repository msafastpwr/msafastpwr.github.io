About Our Chapter

Bridging Classroom Learning with Industry Engineering

Microsoft Student Ambassadors FAST Peshawar is the campus technical society dedicated to fostering real-world engineering skills, cloud literacy, and technological leadership.

Our Mission

Empower every student at FAST-NUCES Peshawar to achieve more with modern cloud native architectures, developer tooling, and collaborative software engineering.

Our Vision

Establish Peshawar as a prominent hub for student open-source contributors, cloud pioneers, and community leaders.

Her Tech Wing

A dedicated institutional initiative within MSA FAST Peshawar creating an inclusive space for female engineers to lead, speak, and innovate.
